# RouteMate AI — Competitor Analysis

## Method and evidence label
This is a desk review of publicly accessible competitor pages, captured as **source-reviewed observations**. Features, coverage, prices, safety processes, and current availability were not independently verified. No competitor is evaluated as better or worse from these observations alone.

| Service | Source-reviewed observation | Relevance / open question |
|---|---|---|
| Quick Ride | Public site presents a ride-sharing/carpooling service proposition. Source: https://quickride.in/ | Validate current matching, commute focus, geographic availability, and data practices before comparison. |
| sRide | Public site presents a social/shared-ride service proposition. Source: https://sride.co/ | Validate identity, trust, group/community mechanics, and operating markets. |
| BlaBlaCar India | Public site presents intercity carpooling/listing and passenger-driver discovery. Source: https://www.blablacar.in/ | Distinguish scheduled/intercity use from RouteMate’s constrained, potentially dynamic matching. |

## Comparison dimensions
RouteMate research should compare only documented behavior: trip type, advance notice, matching constraints, capacity, detour policy, cancellation handling, safety and identity affordances, accessibility, explanation, privacy controls, geography, and user feedback. Unknowns remain unknown until direct testing or primary documentation.

## Differentiation hypothesis
RouteMate’s research contribution is an auditable, constraint-explicit matcher with reproducible evaluation and fairness/uncertainty reporting. This is a hypothesis about project design, not a claim of market novelty or superiority.

## Competitive test plan
Before any public comparison, archive dated source pages, define identical scenarios, seek permission for automated access, and report missing observations. Do not scrape private or authenticated data. A competitor feature matrix must distinguish “documented,” “observed in an authorized test,” and “unknown.”
